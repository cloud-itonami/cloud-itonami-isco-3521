## Customer

- other cloud-itonami vertical businesses wanting a news widget
- independent media/podcast producers

- small broadcasters

## Offer

- daily video/audio news-digest production sourced from kawaraban's mirror feed
- syndication API subscription (embeddable "world news ticker" widget with mandatory
  attribution + link-out)
- white-label editorial curation-as-a-service
- studio/production-booth robot operations (camera framing, audio leveling, asset
  transcode)

## Revenue

- syndication API subscription tiers
- per-episode production fee
- white-label licensing fee

## Trust Controls

- no full-text reproduction of kawaraban-mirrored articles (only kawaraban's own
  bounded excerpt may be quoted, with attribution+link preserved)
- no fabricated sourcing
- no impersonating kawaraban or any mirrored outlet
- every derivative product auditable back to its source kawaraban article ID
- human sign-off required for any studio robot safety-critical action
